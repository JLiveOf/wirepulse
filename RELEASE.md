# WirePulse 4.4.0

- A dedicated What’s New screen with illustrated features and bilingual release notes, bundled for offline use. After updates, the glass splash expands directly into these notes.
- Fresh installations show initial settings first, followed by What’s New in the same window. Updates preserve completed setup and do not ask for those choices again.
- New background music during initial settings and What’s New: the introduction plays once, then a separately scheduled sustain section loops continuously. Moving between pages keeps the same playback session.
- Music can be muted without restarting its introduction and stops when the welcome window closes. Ordinary daily splashes keep their existing short startup sound.
- Release checks require current-version English and Spanish notes and verify that all packages contain the music and note resources.
